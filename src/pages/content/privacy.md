# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Habitability ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:

- Email address
- Display name and username
- Profile photo (optional)
- Your timezone (for reminder scheduling)
- Account identifier and authentication information, including information returned by Google or Apple if you use their sign-in options

### Habit Data
We collect the habits you create and track:

- Habit names, descriptions, icons, and colors
- Completion dates and times
- Streak information and goals
- Reminder schedules and preferences
- Privacy settings for each habit

### Social Data
If you use our social features, we collect:

- Friend connections and requests
- Activity feed data (habit completions shared with friends)
- Likes and reactions on activities
- Nudges, including the message you write, sender, recipient, and related habit

### Device Information
We collect device information to provide push notifications and operate the app:

- Push messaging tokens and installation identifiers, linked to your account
- Device platform (iOS or Android)
- Generic device name and last-active time
- Technical information processed by our service providers, such as IP address, app and operating-system version, and device model

### Usage Analytics and Diagnostics
We use Firebase Analytics for usage events, Crashlytics for crash diagnostics, and Firebase Performance Monitoring for performance measurements. These can include:

- Feature usage and engagement
- App and network performance metrics
- Crash reports, error categories, and stack traces
- Session duration and frequency

Analytics and crash reports can be associated with your account's unique identifier. Performance data uses a Firebase installation identifier, which can be associated with your account through the app's device registration. This is pseudonymous, account-linked data, not anonymous data. These services also process installation and device information.

Analytics and Performance Monitoring derive general location, such as country and, for Analytics, region or city, from your IP address. Habitability does not request GPS location for these features. Collection is enabled in production releases; the app currently has no analytics opt-out setting.

### Subscription Data
If you subscribe to premium features:

- Account-linked subscriber identifier
- Subscription status and entitlements
- Purchase and transaction information processed by the app store and RevenueCat

### Communications
We process your email address and authentication email content to deliver account messages, such as verification and password-reset emails. If you contact us for support or send feedback, we receive the information you choose to include. In-app feedback is linked to your account and includes your app version, platform, and the context in which the feedback prompt appeared.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain the Habitability service
- Send push notifications and reminders
- Enable social features with your friends
- Process subscription purchases
- Analyze app usage to improve features
- Fix bugs and technical issues
- Authenticate accounts and investigate security issues
- Respond to your support requests

## Data Sharing

**We do not sell your personal information.**

We may share your information:

- **With other app users:** Your username, display name, and profile photo can be visible in user searches and profiles, including before you become friends. Profile photos use public image links that anyone with the link can view.
- **With friends you connect with:** Habit and activity visibility depends on your sharing settings. Friend requests, likes, and nudges are visible to the people involved. Recipients may retain copies of content you share.
- **With service providers:** Third-party services listed above that help us operate the app
- **For legal reasons:** When required by law or to protect our rights

## Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** We retain account, habit, and social records to provide the service while your account is active.
- **Account deletion:** Successful in-app deletion removes your account and associated active app records. Profile-photo removal is also attempted; a storage error may require follow-up. Deletion does not immediately erase every copy in backups, logs, or records held by service providers.
- **Recovery and provider records:** Encrypted database and photo backups are kept separately for recovery and are not automatically erased by in-app account deletion. Authentication, security, email-delivery, analytics, crash, and purchase records may remain separately. Retention varies by record type and provider; we do not specify a single deletion period for these records.
- **Copies outside the app:** Signing out is not an account-deletion request. Files you export, copies shared with others, and records held by the app store are outside the in-app deletion process.

## Your Rights and Choices

The app offers these controls:

- **Access your data:** View your habits, completions, and profile in the app
- **Export habits:** Premium users can export habits and completion history as JSON from Profile > Data Management. This export does not include all account, social, or provider records.
- **Delete your account:** Use Profile > Account > Delete Account, subject to the retention details above
- **Control privacy:** Set individual habits as private, visible to all friends, or shared with specific friends
- **Manage friends:** Remove a friend from your friend list
- **Manage notifications:** Use Profile > Notifications for reminder, category, and quiet-hours preferences, and your device settings for notification permission. These settings do not disable analytics.

You can contact privacy@habitability.app to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, including information outside the in-app export. These requests do not require a premium subscription. Your rights may vary depending on where you live.

## Data Deletion Instructions

To delete your account:

1. Open Habitability
2. Go to Profile
3. Scroll to the Account section
4. Tap "Delete Account"
5. Confirm by entering your username

The app signs you out after successful deletion. If deletion fails or you need help with retained records, contact privacy@habitability.app. The backup and provider retention described above still applies. Account deletion does not cancel an app-store subscription; manage that separately through Apple or Google.

## Children's Privacy

Habitability is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## Security

We use security measures including:

- Authentication with session tokens
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Database access controls, including row-level security
- Encryption and restricted access for recovery backups

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in countries other than your own by us and our service providers. Provider privacy and data-processing information is linked above.
