# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** December 20, 2025

Habitability ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Email address
- Display name and username
- Profile photo (optional)
- Your timezone (for reminder scheduling)

### Habit Data
We collect the habits you create and track:
- Habit names, descriptions, icons, and colors
- Completion dates and times
- Streak information and goals
- Reminder schedules and preferences
- Privacy settings for each habit

### Social Data
If you use our social features, we collect:
- Friend connections and requests
- Activity feed data (habit completions shared with friends)
- Likes and reactions on activities
- Nudges (motivational messages between friends)

### Device Information
We collect device information to provide push notifications:
- Device token (FCM/APNS token)
- Device platform (iOS or Android)
- Generic device name

### Usage Analytics
We use Firebase Analytics to understand how you use the app:
- Feature usage and engagement
- App performance metrics
- Crash reports and error logs
- Session duration and frequency

### Subscription Data
If you subscribe to premium features:
- Subscription status and entitlements
- Purchase history (processed by Apple/Google)

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the Habitability service
- Send push notifications and reminders
- Enable social features with your friends
- Process subscription purchases
- Analyze app usage to improve features
- Fix bugs and technical issues
- Respond to your support requests

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

| Service | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Supabase** | Database, authentication, file storage | Account data, habits, social data |
| **Firebase Analytics** | Usage analytics | Anonymous usage events, crash reports |
| **Firebase Cloud Messaging** | Push notifications | Device tokens |
| **RevenueCat** | Subscription management | Subscriber ID, purchase data |
| **Google Sign-In** | Authentication | Email, name (from your Google account) |
| **Apple Sign-In** | Authentication | Email, name (from your Apple ID) |

These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of your data.

## Data Sharing

**We do not sell your personal information.**

We may share your information:
- **With friends you connect with:** Your shared habits, completions, and profile are visible to friends based on your privacy settings
- **With service providers:** Third-party services listed above that help us operate the app
- **For legal reasons:** When required by law or to protect our rights

## Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** Your data is retained while your account is active
- **Account deletion:** When you delete your account, all your data is immediately and permanently deleted from our servers
- **Cached data:** Local device caches are cleared upon sign out

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:
- **Access your data:** View your habits, completions, and profile in the app
- **Export your data:** Download all your data as a JSON file from Settings > Data Management
- **Delete your data:** Delete your account and all associated data from Settings > Account > Delete Account
- **Control privacy:** Set individual habits as private, visible to all friends, or shared with specific friends
- **Manage notifications:** Control notification preferences in the app settings
- **Opt out of analytics:** Disable analytics tracking in your device settings

## Data Deletion Instructions

To delete your account and all associated data:
1. Open Habitability
2. Go to Profile > Settings
3. Scroll to Account section
4. Tap "Delete Account"
5. Confirm by entering your username
6. Your data will be immediately and permanently deleted

## Children's Privacy

Habitability is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:
- Secure authentication with session tokens
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Row-level security on database tables
- Biometric authentication option (Face ID/Touch ID)

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Last updated" date
- Sending an in-app notification for significant changes
